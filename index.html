Who we are

Egyptian Group for Trading & Manufacturing (Egy Group) is an Egyptian company specializing in the manufacturing of plastic piping systems and fittings. We provide integrated solutions for water supply and drainage networks, with a strong commitment to quality, reliability, and excellence throughout every stage of the manufacturing process. Leveraging advanced production lines and modern industrial technologies, we manufacture high-quality PVC and PPR products that meet the demands of residential, commercial, and industrial projects. Our products are engineered to deliver durability, efficiency, and long-term performance. Every product undergoes rigorous inspection and testing to ensure compliance with applicable quality standards and technical specifications, giving our customers confidence in the reliability and performance of our solutions across a wide range of applications. At Egy Group, we go beyond manufacturing. We strive to build long-term partnerships with our customers by delivering consistent quality, expert technical support, and professional service, making us a trusted partner for infrastructure and urban development projects in Egypt and beyond.
