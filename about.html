About Egy Group

Two and a half decades of piping Egypt's infrastructure.

From a single extrusion line in 1998 to a full-range pipe systems manufacturer serving contractors, developers and utilities nationwide.

Our mission

Reliable materials, delivered on schedule.

Egy Group Company exists to give engineers and contractors a pipe system they don't have to think twice about — consistent dimensions, verified pressure ratings, and stock available when the programme demands it.

We manufacture across three polymer systems — PVC-U, PPR and PPH — so a single supplier can cover drainage, potable water and industrial process lines on the same project.

Our vision

To be the reference pipe manufacturer of the Nile Delta industrial corridor.

We aim to expand our extrusion capacity and export footprint while holding the same tolerances that earned us our first municipal contract in 2003.
