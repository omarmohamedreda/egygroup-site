What we do

Support that goes past the factory gate.

From the first spec conversation to the last delivery on site, our team stays involved so your project keeps moving.

Our services

Custom Manufacturing

Non-standard diameters, colours, lengths and fitting configurations produced against your project's bill of quantities, with minimum order quantities discussed up front.

Technical Consultation

Our engineers review drawings and BOQs to confirm pipe class, pressure rating and fitting selection before you commit to a tender price.

Logistics & Delivery

Site-scheduled deliveries across all 27 governorates using our own fleet, with staged deliveries available for phased construction programmes.

After-Sales Support

Installation guidance, warranty handling and replacement-part sourcing for as long as your system is in service.
